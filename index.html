About EOSIAL Viewer

Interactive viewer for satellite-derived environmental variables produced by the EOSIAL Laboratory at Sapienza University of Rome.

Available layers:

  • LFMC — Live Fuel Moisture Content estimated from Sentinel-2 L2A, Landsat 8/9, VIIRS VNP09H1 or Sentinel-3 Synergy 10-Day aggregate imagery.
  • Fire Hotspots — Near-real-time active fire detections from MSG/MTG satellites (SFIDE algorithm).
