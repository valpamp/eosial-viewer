About EOSIAL Active Fire Viewer

Interactive active fire viewer for satellite wildfire management products produced by the EOSIAL Laboratory at Sapienza University of Rome.

Available layers:

  • SFIDE Hotspots — Near-real-time active fire detections from MSG/MTG satellites (SFIDE algorithm).
  • FIRMS Hotspots — External NASA FIRMS near-real-time detections for comparison with SFIDE products.
  • Sentinel-3 Hotspots — External Sentinel-3 SLSTR near-real-time FRP detections for comparison with geostationary products.
  • MTG-FIR Hotspots — External official EUMETSAT MTG-FIR detections for comparison with SFIDE products.
